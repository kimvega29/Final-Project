Building a Complete Fitness Routine

A balanced fitness program should include strength training, cardiovascular activity, good nutrition, and enough recovery.

  1. Strength Training
    • Squats or leg press
    • Chest exercises
    • Back exercises
    • Shoulder exercises
  2. Cardiovascular Training
    • Walking
    • Running
    • Cycling
    • Stair climbing
  3. Recovery
    • Sleep 7–9 hours
    • Drink enough water
    • Take rest days
    • Stretch regularly

CONSISTENCY

Small Actions Create Big Results

The best workout program is not necessarily the most complicated program. A successful program is one that a person can follow consistently.

Progress comes from gradually improving strength, endurance, nutrition, and daily habits.
